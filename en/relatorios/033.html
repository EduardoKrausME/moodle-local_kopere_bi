REPORT 033

Platform usage by teachers

Per-teacher summary of associated visible courses, aggregated student counts, and recorded time and visits on the platform.

Report purpose

Per-teacher summary of associated visible courses, aggregated student counts, and recorded time and visits on the platform.

Why use this report?

Helps administrators and course coordinators examine teachers' recorded use of courses alongside the numbers of associated students as a basis for further analysis and support.

Available filters Visualization and SQL Limitations

Available filters

There are no dynamic SQL parameters for course, teacher, student, cohort, or date range. The query considers teachers assigned role ID 3 in a course context (contextlevel = 50), visible courses, and users who are neither deleted nor suspended and are not guest.

Student counts use role ID 5 and visible courses. Time and visits are retrieved from mdl_local_kopere_bi_tracking and mdl_local_kopere_bi_track_log where log records are linked to tracking records.

Visualization and full original SQL

The report contains one table visualization and one SQL query, with cache and refresh settings and Prism.js syntax highlighting. No separate PostgreSQL variant is provided.

1. Platform usage by teachers · Table

The per-teacher table shows the teacher's name, number of distinct courses, sum of student counts across courses, and total time and visits derived from Kopere BI tracking logs.

Cache: 1h · Refresh: none.

Full original SQL

SELECT kbi_u.id,
       CONCAT(kbi_u.firstname, ' ', kbi_u.lastname)                               AS name,
       COUNT(DISTINCT kbi_ctx.instanceid)                                     as courses,
       SUM(v.learners)                                                    as learners,
       MAX(CASE WHEN lit.timespend IS NULL THEN 0 ELSE lit.timespend END) AS timespend,
       MAX(CASE WHEN lit.visits IS NULL THEN 0 ELSE lit.visits END)       AS visits
FROM mdl_role_assignments kbi_ra
       JOIN mdl_context kbi_ctx ON kbi_ctx.id = kbi_ra.contextid AND kbi_ctx.contextlevel = 50
       JOIN mdl_course kbi_c ON kbi_ctx.instanceid = kbi_c.id AND kbi_c.visible = 1
       JOIN mdl_user kbi_u ON kbi_u.id = kbi_ra.userid
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT kbi_ctx.instanceid, COUNT(DISTINCT kbi_ra.userid) as learners
                  FROM mdl_role_assignments kbi_ra
                         JOIN mdl_context kbi_ctx ON kbi_ctx.id = kbi_ra.contextid
                         JOIN mdl_course kbi_c ON kbi_ctx.instanceid = kbi_c.id AND kbi_c.visible = 1
                  WHERE kbi_ra.userid > 0
                    AND kbi_ra.roleid = 5
                  GROUP BY kbi_ctx.instanceid) v ON v.instanceid = kbi_ctx.instanceid
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT kbi_lit.userid,
                         SUM(CASE WHEN kbi_lil.timespend IS NULL THEN 0 ELSE kbi_lil.timespend END) AS timespend,
                         SUM(CASE WHEN kbi_lil.visits IS NULL THEN 0 ELSE kbi_lil.visits END)       AS visits
                  FROM mdl_role_assignments kbi_ra
                         JOIN mdl_context kbi_ctx ON kbi_ctx.contextlevel = 50 AND kbi_ctx.id = kbi_ra.contextid
                         JOIN mdl_course kbi_c ON kbi_c.id = kbi_ctx.instanceid AND kbi_c.visible = 1
                         LEFT JOIN mdl_local_kopere_bi_tracking kbi_lit
                           ON kbi_lit.userid = kbi_ra.userid AND kbi_lit.courseid = kbi_ctx.instanceid
                         LEFT JOIN mdl_local_kopere_bi_track_log kbi_lil ON kbi_lil.trackid = kbi_lit.id
                  WHERE kbi_lil.id > 0
                    AND kbi_ra.roleid = 3
                  GROUP BY kbi_lit.userid) lit ON lit.userid = kbi_ra.userid
WHERE kbi_u.id > 0
  AND kbi_u.deleted = 0
  AND kbi_u.suspended = 0
  AND kbi_u.username != 'guest'
  AND kbi_ra.roleid = 3
GROUP BY kbi_u.id, kbi_u.firstname, kbi_u.lastname

Limitations and cautions

  • The learners column sums students associated with the teacher's courses. A person in several courses may be counted more than once.
  • Time and visits are aggregated per teacher in the subquery, and the outer query applies MAX to those values repeated across their course rows. These are not measurements for each individual course.
  • The total recorded usage depends on records in the tracking and log tables, whose collection and retention may vary. Missing records are converted to zero in the final query.
  • Student counts come from role ID 5 assignments without directly checking enrolment status, so they may differ from active enrolment counts.
  • The join between context and course in the student subquery does not explicitly require contextlevel = 50. Validate this on installations with customized contexts and assignments.
  • Without a date filter, these indicators reflect available history and do not establish teaching quality or continuous attention.
  • No separate PostgreSQL variant is supplied, and user-level information should be available only to authorized roles.
