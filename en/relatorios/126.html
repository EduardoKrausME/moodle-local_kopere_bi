REPORT 126

Forum posts by weekday and time of day

Forum posts, contributors, and discussions started by weekday and UTC time slot over the past 180 days.

Report purpose

A table by course, forum, weekday (0–6), and UTC time slot, showing total posts, distinct contributors, discussions started, and average posts per contributor. Results are ordered by course, forum name, weekday, and time slot, and limited to 500 groups.

Why use this report?

Identify temporal patterns in forum participation by course and activity, comparing weekdays, UTC time slots, posts, contributors, and discussions started.

Available filters Visualization and SQL Calculation criteria Limitations

Available filters

There are no dynamic SQL filters for course, forum, participant, weekday, or time period. The fixed time condition is p.created >= UNIX_TIMESTAMP() - 15552000, corresponding to the 180 consecutive 24-hour days preceding execution.

The query excludes users for whom u.deleted = 1, but does not explicitly filter suspended accounts, hidden courses, hidden forums, or course roles. Results are limited to 500 groups, ordered by course, forum, weekday, and time slot.

Visualization and full original SQL

The report contains one table visualization with the full original SQL, caching and refresh settings. Prism.js provides syntax highlighting. No separate PostgreSQL variant was found in master; no nonexistent query has been introduced.

1. Forum posts by weekday and time of day · Table

Table by course, forum, weekday (0–6), and UTC time slot, including total posts, distinct contributors, discussions started, and average posts per contributor. Groups are ordered by course, forum, weekday, and time slot, with a maximum of 500.

Cache: 1h · Refresh: none.

Full original SQL

SELECT CONCAT(x.courseid, '-', x.forumid, '-', x.weekday_number, '-', x.period) AS id,
       x.courseid, x.course, x.forumid, x.forum_name, x.weekday_number, x.period,
       COUNT(*) AS posts, COUNT(DISTINCT x.userid) AS contributors,
       SUM(x.new_discussion) AS discussions,
       ROUND(1.0 * COUNT(*) / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT x.userid), 0), 2)
           AS posts_per_contributor
  FROM (SELECT c.id AS courseid, c.fullname AS course, forum.id AS forumid,
               forum.name AS forum_name, p.userid,
               MOD(FLOOR(p.created / 86400.0) + 3, 7) AS weekday_number,
               CASE WHEN MOD(FLOOR(p.created / 3600.0), 24) < 6 THEN '00:00-05:59'
                    WHEN MOD(FLOOR(p.created / 3600.0), 24) < 12 THEN '06:00-11:59'
                    WHEN MOD(FLOOR(p.created / 3600.0), 24) < 17 THEN '12:00-16:59'
                    ELSE '17:00-23:59' END AS period,
               CASE WHEN p.parent = 0 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END AS new_discussion
          FROM mdl_forum_posts p
          JOIN mdl_forum_discussions disc ON disc.id = p.discussion
          JOIN mdl_forum forum ON forum.id = disc.forum
          JOIN mdl_course c ON c.id = forum.course
          JOIN mdl_user u ON u.id = p.userid AND u.deleted = 0
         WHERE p.created >= UNIX_TIMESTAMP() - 15552000) x
 GROUP BY x.courseid, x.course, x.forumid, x.forum_name, x.weekday_number, x.period
 ORDER BY x.course, x.forum_name, x.weekday_number, x.period
 LIMIT 500

Calculation criteria and behavior

The weekday is calculated using MOD(FLOOR(p.created / 86400.0) + 3, 7) based on UTC. The numbering is 0 = Monday, 1 = Tuesday, through 6 = Sunday. Time slots are determined by the post timestamp in UTC.

The posts field uses COUNT(*); contributors uses COUNT(DISTINCT userid); and discussions sums records with p.parent = 0 to identify posts that start a discussion. posts_per_contributor divides posts by the number of distinct contributors in the group, rounded to two decimal places.

The UTC time slots are 00:00–05:59, 06:00–11:59, 12:00–16:59, and 17:00–23:59.

Limitations and cautions

  • The four time slots have unequal lengths: 6, 6, 5, and 7 hours. Comparing their raw counts can therefore overstate activity in longer slots.
  • Days and times are in UTC, not the user’s or institution’s local time zone. Convert them before interpreting local patterns.
  • Distinct contributors are counted within each forum/weekday/time-slot group. Summing groups may count the same person more than once.
  • An initial post identified by parent = 0 counts both as a post and as a discussion started.
  • Recorded posts do not demonstrate the educational quality of interactions, whether topics were read, or sustained engagement in discussions.
  • The query excludes deleted accounts but does not restrict participants to students or explicitly exclude hidden courses, forums, or posts.
  • The 180-day window moves with query execution, and the first and last days can be incomplete. The 500-group limit may omit some forums.

The SQL was reproduced verbatim from the original JSON definition in master. No queries were executed on Moodle during this documentation work. Before institutional use, verify database compatibility, interpretation of the data, and access permissions.
