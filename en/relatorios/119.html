REPORT 119

Student participation versus course average

Compares each student's recorded actions over the past 30 days with the course average in the same analytics batch; actions do not measure learning.

Report purpose

Table by student and course with actions over the past 30 days, average actions for the course, the percentage ratio of individual actions to that average, individual progress, and average course progress. It sorts by absolute action count, course, and surname, and returns up to 500 rows.

Why use this report?

Help tutors and administrators identify differences between a student's recorded participation and the average for all analyzed learners in the same course, while interpreting actions alongside average progress.

Available filters Visualization and SQL Calculation criteria Limitations

Available filters

There are no dynamic SQL parameters for course, student, cohort, or period. Student records and course averages both come from the latest MAX(batchid) available in mdl_local_kopere_bi_engage.

The 30-day window is already built into the actions30 field calculated in the analytics batch, rather than selected in this query. The SQL limits the result to 500 records, sorted by e.actions30 DESC.

Visualization and full original SQL

The report has one table visualization, with complete original SQL, cache and refresh settings, and Prism.js syntax highlighting. There is no separate PostgreSQL variant in master for this definition, so no nonexistent query has been added.

1. Student participation versus course average · Table

Table by student and course showing actions in the past 30 days, course-average actions, percentage ratio to that average, individual progress, and average course progress. Rows are sorted by absolute action count, course, and surname, with a maximum of 500.

Cache: 1h · Refresh: none.

Full original SQL

SELECT e.id, e.courseid, c.fullname AS course, e.userid, u.firstname, u.lastname,
       e.actions30, ROUND(peer.class_actions_avg, 2) AS class_actions_avg,
       CASE WHEN peer.class_actions_avg > 0 THEN ROUND(100.0 * e.actions30 / peer.class_actions_avg, 2)
            ELSE NULL END AS activity_ratio,
       e.progress, ROUND(peer.class_progress_avg, 2) AS class_progress_avg
  FROM mdl_local_kopere_bi_engage e
  JOIN mdl_user u ON u.id = e.userid
  JOIN mdl_course c ON c.id = e.courseid
  JOIN (SELECT courseid, AVG(actions30) AS class_actions_avg, AVG(progress) AS class_progress_avg
          FROM mdl_local_kopere_bi_engage
         WHERE batchid = (SELECT MAX(batchid) FROM mdl_local_kopere_bi_engage)
         GROUP BY courseid) peer ON peer.courseid = e.courseid
 WHERE e.batchid = (SELECT MAX(batchid) FROM mdl_local_kopere_bi_engage)
 ORDER BY e.actions30 DESC, c.fullname, u.lastname
 LIMIT 500

Calculation criteria and behavior

class_actions_avg is the AVG(actions30) of records in the course, grouped by courseid in the most recent analytics batch. The same subquery calculates class_progress_avg using AVG(progress). Averages are rounded to two decimal places in the table.

The activity_ratio is ROUND(100.0 * e.actions30 / class_actions_avg, 2) when the average is greater than zero; otherwise it is NULL. A value of 100 means actions equal to the average, 150 means 1.5 times the average, and 50 means half the average. It is neither a completion rate nor a percentage change with 100 subtracted.

Limitations and cautions

  • Recorded actions represent interactions; they do not measure learning, study quality, complete reading of materials, or grades.
  • Averages represent records in the course's analytics batch. There is no explicit filter for active enrolments, suspended accounts, or visible courses.
  • The calculation is not segmented by a Moodle group or class: “course average” means aggregated records within the same course.
  • If the average action count is zero or NULL, activity_ratio is NULL. This does not mean the student's performance is zero.
  • The 500 rows are chosen by individual action volume, not by the greatest difference above or below the average. Students with few actions may be omitted.
  • The accuracy and freshness of actions30 and progress depend on processing that populates the latest analytics batch.
  • The table contains student identities and individual metrics. Restrict access and exports according to institutional policies and Moodle permissions.

The query is reproduced verbatim from the original JSON in master. It was not executed in Moodle during this documentation work; before institutional use, validate database compatibility, access permissions, and metric interpretation.
