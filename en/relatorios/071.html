REPORT 071

Forum activity details

Details forum discussions in a course, their authors, reply counts, and access and time indicators recorded by Kopere BI.

Report purpose

Details forum discussions in a course, their authors, reply counts, and access and time indicators recorded by Kopere BI.

Why use this report?

Helps educational teams identify discussions, review participation through replies, and examine tracking records associated with forum modules.

Available filters Visualization and SQL Limitations

Available filters

The dynamic SQL filter is Course, represented by :courseid and used once in kbi_f.course = :courseid.

Fixed criteria include a positive forum ID, a course context (contextlevel = 50), and role ID 5 assignments in the tracking aggregation subquery. There are no filters for time period, discussion, author, course or module visibility, or account status.

Visualization and full original SQL

One table visualization includes the complete original SQL query, cache and refresh settings, and Prism.js syntax highlighting. No separate PostgreSQL variant is provided in the source.

1. Forum activity details · Table

The table shows each forum discussion's ID, course, forum, discussion title, author, earliest date among its posts, distinct reply count, and aggregate counts of tracked users and time recorded for the forum module.

Cache: 1h · Refresh: none.

Full original SQL

SELECT kbi_fd.id,
       kbi_c.id                                                             AS courseid,
       kbi_c.fullname                                                       AS course_name,
       kbi_f.name                                                           AS forum,
       kbi_fd.name                                                          AS discusion,
       kbi_u.id                                                             AS userid,
       kbi_u.firstname,
       kbi_u.lastname,
       MIN(kbi_fp.created)                                                  AS time_started,
       MIN(stat.students_accesed)                                       AS students_accesed,
       MIN(stat.timespend)                                              AS timespend,
       COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN kbi_fp.parent > 0 THEN kbi_fp.id ELSE NULL END) AS posts
FROM mdl_forum kbi_f
       JOIN mdl_forum_discussions kbi_fd ON kbi_fd.forum = kbi_f.id
       JOIN mdl_user kbi_u ON kbi_u.id = kbi_fd.userid
       JOIN mdl_course kbi_c ON kbi_c.id = kbi_f.course
       LEFT JOIN mdl_forum_posts kbi_fp ON kbi_fp.discussion = kbi_fd.id
       JOIN mdl_modules kbi_m ON kbi_m.name = 'forum'
       JOIN mdl_course_modules kbi_cm ON kbi_cm.instance = kbi_f.id AND kbi_cm.module = kbi_m.id
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT kbi_lit.param                  AS module,
                         COUNT(DISTINCT kbi_lit.userid) AS students_accesed,
                         SUM(kbi_lit.timespend)         AS timespend
                  FROM mdl_context kbi_ctx
                         JOIN mdl_role_assignments kbi_ra ON kbi_ra.contextid = kbi_ctx.id
                         LEFT JOIN mdl_local_kopere_bi_tracking kbi_lit
                           ON kbi_lit.page = 'module' AND kbi_lit.courseid = kbi_ctx.instanceid AND kbi_lit.userid = kbi_ra.userid
                  WHERE kbi_ctx.contextlevel = 50
                    AND kbi_ra.roleid = 5
                  GROUP BY kbi_lit.param) stat ON stat.module = kbi_cm.id
WHERE kbi_f.id > 0
  AND kbi_f.course = :courseid
GROUP BY kbi_fd.id, kbi_c.id, kbi_f.name, kbi_u.id

Limitations and cautions

  • The posts column counts only posts with kbi_fp.parent > 0, namely replies to discussions. It excludes the discussion's initial post.
  • The time_started column uses MIN(kbi_fp.created), the earliest date among associated posts. It may be NULL when no matching posts exist; it is not taken directly from mdl_forum_discussions.timecreated.
  • The students_accesed and timespend indicators come from Kopere BI tracking for page = 'module', not confirmed visits to a particular discussion. The same module-level metrics may be repeated across multiple discussions in the forum.
  • The tracking subquery aggregates by kbi_lit.param without including courseid in the grouping key. If parameter values overlap across contexts, totals may mix records. Verify that the parameter corresponds to the CMID.
  • The COUNT(DISTINCT kbi_lit.userid) counts tracked users assigned role ID 5 in the corresponding context without requiring active enrolment. The students_accesed column does not indicate whether posts were read or understood.
  • The main query does not require course or module visibility or check whether the discussion author is deleted or suspended.
  • The reply count may include authors with various roles. The role ID 5 condition applies only to the tracking subquery, not to the posts count.
  • There is no dynamic date filter, and the report does not provide a separate PostgreSQL variant.
