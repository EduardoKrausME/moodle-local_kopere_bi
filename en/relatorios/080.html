REPORT 080

Assignment submission status

Lists participants whose assignment submission records in the selected course were modified after the deadline or have no modification date, including profile details and grades.

Report purpose

Lists participants whose assignment submission records in the selected course were modified after the deadline or have no modification date, including profile details and grades.

Why use this report?

Helps teachers and administrators review assignments with indications of changes after the deadline and identify participants without a corresponding submission record for further investigation.

Available filters Visualization and SQL Limitations

Available filters

The dynamic SQL filter is Course, represented by :courseid and used once in kbi_c.id = :courseid.

Fixed conditions require an existing assignment, a visible course and module, and a user who is neither deleted nor suspended and is not guest. The main condition selects kbi_s.timemodified > kbi_a.duedate or kbi_s.timemodified IS NULL. There are no dynamic filters for period, enrolment status, or participant role.

Visualization and full original SQL

One table visualization contains one complete SQL query, cache and refresh settings, and Prism.js syntax highlighting. The source has no separate PostgreSQL variant file.

1. Assignment submission status · Table

The table has one row per assignment and user with an associated course enrolment record, including assignment name and deadline, course, submission status and modification date, participant identification and contact information, institutional fields, and final grade converted to a percentage when the maximum grade is positive.

Cache: 1h · Refresh: none.

Full original SQL

SELECT kbi_a.name                               as assignment,
       kbi_a.duedate,
       kbi_c.fullname                           as course,
       kbi_s.status,
       kbi_s.timemodified                       AS submitted,
       kbi_u.email,
       kbi_u.idnumber,
       kbi_u.phone1,
       kbi_u.phone2,
       kbi_u.institution,
       kbi_u.department,
       kbi_u.address,
       kbi_u.city,
       kbi_u.country,
       kbi_u.firstname,
       kbi_u.lastname,
       ROUND((CASE
                WHEN kbi_gg.rawgrademax > 0 THEN (kbi_gg.finalgrade / kbi_gg.rawgrademax) * 100
                ELSE kbi_gg.finalgrade END), 0) AS grade
FROM mdl_assign kbi_a
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT kbi_e.courseid, kbi_ue.userid
                  FROM mdl_user_enrolments kbi_ue, mdl_enrol kbi_e
                  WHERE kbi_e.id = kbi_ue.enrolid
                  GROUP BY kbi_e.courseid, kbi_ue.userid) ue ON ue.courseid = kbi_a.course
       LEFT JOIN mdl_user kbi_u ON kbi_u.id = ue.userid
       LEFT JOIN mdl_assign_submission kbi_s ON kbi_s.assignment = kbi_a.id AND kbi_s.userid = kbi_u.id
       LEFT JOIN mdl_course kbi_c ON kbi_c.id = kbi_a.course
       LEFT JOIN mdl_modules kbi_m ON kbi_m.name = 'assign'
       LEFT JOIN mdl_course_modules kbi_cm ON kbi_cm.instance = kbi_a.id AND kbi_cm.module = kbi_m.id
       LEFT JOIN mdl_grade_items kbi_gi
         ON kbi_gi.courseid = kbi_c.id AND kbi_gi.itemtype = 'mod' AND kbi_gi.itemmodule = kbi_m.name AND kbi_gi.iteminstance = kbi_cm.instance
       LEFT JOIN mdl_grade_grades kbi_gg ON kbi_gg.itemid = kbi_gi.id AND kbi_gg.userid = kbi_u.id
WHERE kbi_a.id > 0
  AND kbi_c.id = :courseid
  AND kbi_u.deleted = 0
  AND kbi_u.suspended = 0
  AND kbi_u.username != 'guest'
  AND kbi_c.visible = 1
  AND kbi_cm.visible = 1
  AND (kbi_s.timemodified > kbi_a.duedate or kbi_s.timemodified IS NULL)
ORDER BY kbi_c.fullname, kbi_a.name, kbi_u.lastname, kbi_u.firstname

Limitations and cautions

  • This selection is not a list of confirmed late submissions. It compares assign_submission.timemodified with assign.duedate, and a modification after the deadline does not necessarily represent the original submission.
  • Records without an associated submission also qualify because of timemodified IS NULL. Submission status and modification date may therefore be empty.
  • The condition does not require kbi_s.status = 'submitted'; drafts or other states may appear if they meet the date condition.
  • If the configured deadline (duedate) is zero, submission modifications with positive timestamps may satisfy the late condition even when the assignment has no effective deadline.
  • The table uses users with enrolment records but does not check current enrolment status or require role ID 5. Teachers and other enrolled roles may appear.
  • The grade column uses the final gradebook grade and converts it to a percentage only when rawgrademax > 0. Missing grades are not the same as zero.
  • The query includes email addresses, institutional identifiers, phone numbers, addresses, and other personal information. Restrict viewing and exports to authorized roles.
  • The query does not verify group criteria, individual deadline extensions, exemptions, or assignment-specific submission rules. No separate PostgreSQL variant is provided.
