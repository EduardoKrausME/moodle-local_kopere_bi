REPORT 031

Teacher participation in forums

Per-teacher table for the selected course showing forum, discussion and reply counts, together with recorded forum usage.

Report purpose

Per-teacher table for the selected course showing forum, discussion and reply counts, together with recorded forum usage.

Why use this report?

Helps coordinators and academic teams compare teachers' recorded participation in forums, including posts authored by teachers and course-wide indicators.

Available filters Visualization and SQL Limitations

Available filters

The available dynamic SQL filter is Course, bound to :courseid once in the WHERE clause.

Fixed criteria require teacher role ID 3 in a course context (contextlevel = 50), a visible course, a user who is neither deleted nor suspended and is not guest. Time and visits are drawn from tracking of forum modules with page = 'module'. There is no teacher, individual forum, or date-range parameter.

Visualization and full original SQL

The report contains one table and one SQL query, with its cache and refresh settings and Prism.js syntax highlighting. No separate PostgreSQL variant is provided for this report.

1. Teacher participation in forums · Table

One row per teacher and course, showing course forum counts, total discussions and replies, discussions and replies authored by the teacher, and time and visits recorded by Kopere BI in forum modules.

Cache: 1h · Refresh: none.

Full original SQL

SELECT MAX(kbi_ra.id)                             AS id,
       kbi_u.firstname,
       kbi_u.lastname,
       kbi_u.email,
       kbi_c.fullname,
       kbi_c.shortname,
       COUNT(distinct kbi_f.id)                   as forums,
       MAX(coalesce(d.discussions, 0))        AS discussions,
       MAX(coalesce(p.posts, 0))              AS posts,
       MAX(coalesce(d1.users_discussions, 0)) AS users_discussions,
       MAX(coalesce(p1.users_posts, 0))       AS users_posts,
       MAX(l.timespend)                       AS timespend,
       MAX(l.visits)                          AS visits
FROM mdl_role_assignments kbi_ra
       JOIN mdl_user kbi_u ON kbi_ra.userid = kbi_u.id
       JOIN mdl_context kbi_ctx ON kbi_ctx.id = kbi_ra.contextid
       JOIN mdl_course kbi_c ON kbi_c.id = kbi_ctx.instanceid
       LEFT JOIN mdl_forum kbi_f ON kbi_f.course = kbi_c.id
       LEFT JOIN mdl_modules kbi_m ON kbi_m.name = 'forum'
       LEFT JOIN mdl_course_modules kbi_cm ON kbi_cm.instance = kbi_f.id AND kbi_cm.module = kbi_m.id
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT course, COUNT(id) AS discussions FROM mdl_forum_discussions kbi_d GROUP BY course) d
         ON d.course = kbi_c.id
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT kbi_d.course, COUNT(kbi_p.id) AS posts
                  FROM mdl_forum_discussions kbi_d
                         JOIN mdl_forum_posts kbi_p ON kbi_p.discussion = kbi_d.id AND kbi_p.parent > 0
                  GROUP BY kbi_d.course) p ON p.course = kbi_c.id
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT COUNT(id) AS users_discussions, course, userid
                  FROM mdl_forum_discussions kbi_d
                  GROUP BY course, userid) d1 ON d1.course = kbi_c.id AND d1.userid = kbi_u.id
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT COUNT(kbi_p.id) AS users_posts, kbi_d.course, kbi_p.userid
                  FROM mdl_forum_discussions kbi_d
                         JOIN mdl_forum_posts kbi_p ON kbi_p.discussion = kbi_d.id AND kbi_p.parent > 0
                  GROUP BY kbi_d.course, kbi_p.userid) p1 ON p1.course = kbi_c.id AND p1.userid = kbi_u.id
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT kbi_l.userid, kbi_l.courseid, SUM(kbi_l.timespend) as timespend, SUM(kbi_l.visits) as visits
                  FROM mdl_local_kopere_bi_tracking kbi_l, mdl_modules kbi_m, mdl_course_modules kbi_cm
                  WHERE kbi_l.page = 'module'
                    and kbi_m.name = 'forum'
                    AND kbi_cm.id = kbi_l.param
                    AND kbi_cm.module = kbi_m.id
                  GROUP BY kbi_l.userid, kbi_l.courseid) l ON l.userid = kbi_u.id AND l.courseid = kbi_c.id
WHERE kbi_ctx.contextlevel = 50
  AND kbi_c.id = :courseid
  AND kbi_u.deleted = 0
  AND kbi_u.suspended = 0
  AND kbi_u.username != 'guest'
  AND kbi_c.visible = 1
  AND kbi_ra.roleid = 3
GROUP BY kbi_u.id, kbi_c.id, kbi_u.firstname, kbi_u.lastname, kbi_u.email, kbi_c.fullname, kbi_c.shortname

Limitations and cautions

  • The forums, discussions, and posts columns contain course-wide aggregates repeated for each teacher. Only users_discussions and users_posts explicitly restrict authorship to the teacher in the row.
  • The posts and users_posts totals count replies with kbi_p.parent > 0. The initial post of each discussion is excluded from these two counts.
  • Per-teacher time and visits depend on data collected in mdl_local_kopere_bi_tracking; empty fields do not prove that no activity occurred.
  • The query applies no time window and does not explicitly filter individual forums by visibility; only course visibility is checked.
  • The teacher role is identified by fixed ID 3, which may not represent every teacher in installations with custom roles.
  • No separate PostgreSQL variant is supplied in this definition. Teacher names and email addresses require appropriate permissions.
