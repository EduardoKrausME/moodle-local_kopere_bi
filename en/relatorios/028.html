REPORT 028

Teacher participation in assignments

Per-teacher table for the selected course, including assignment totals, aggregated submission, completion and grading indicators, and time and visits tracked in assignment activities.

Report purpose

Per-teacher table for the selected course, including assignment totals, aggregated submission, completion and grading indicators, and time and visits tracked in assignment activities.

Why use this report?

Helps coordinators and managers assess the number of available assignments in a course alongside teachers' activity access records, supporting further investigation of follow-up practices.

Available filters Visualization and SQL Limitations

Available filters

The only dynamic SQL parameter is :courseid, used three times: to aggregate course assignments, aggregate tracking for assignment modules, and select the main course.

Other criteria are fixed: teachers assigned role ID 3 in the course context (contextlevel = 50), a visible course, accounts that are neither deleted nor suspended, and non-guest accounts. Tracking includes page = 'module' linked to assign modules. There are no parameters for teacher, individual assignment, or date range.

Visualization and full original SQL

The report contains one table visualization and one SQL query, with cache, refresh settings, and Prism.js syntax highlighting. No separate PostgreSQL variant is provided in the catalog.

1. Teacher participation in assignments · Table

The table combines teacher name and email with course details, assignment count, assignments with recorded completions, submissions and grades, and time and visits tracked for that teacher in assignment modules. All four assignment counts are aggregated by course; time and visits are calculated by teacher.

Cache: 1h · Refresh: none.

Full original SQL

SELECT kbi_ra.id AS id,
       kbi_u.firstname,
       kbi_u.lastname,
       kbi_u.email,
       kbi_c.fullname,
       kbi_c.shortname,
       stat.assignments,
       stat.completed,
       stat.submissions,
       stat.grades,
       l.timespend,
       visits
FROM mdl_role_assignments kbi_ra
       JOIN mdl_user kbi_u ON kbi_ra.userid = kbi_u.id
       JOIN mdl_context kbi_ctx ON kbi_ctx.id = kbi_ra.contextid
       JOIN mdl_course kbi_c ON kbi_c.id = kbi_ctx.instanceid
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT kbi_a.course                           AS courseid,
                         COUNT(distinct kbi_a.id)               AS assignments,
                         COUNT(distinct kbi_cmc.coursemoduleid) AS completed,
                         COUNT(distinct kbi_s.assignment)       AS submissions,
                         COUNT(distinct kbi_g.assignment)       AS grades
                  FROM mdl_assign kbi_a
                         LEFT JOIN mdl_modules kbi_m ON kbi_m.name = 'assign'
                         LEFT JOIN mdl_course_modules kbi_cm ON kbi_cm.instance = kbi_a.id AND kbi_cm.module = kbi_m.id
                         LEFT JOIN mdl_course_modules_completion kbi_cmc ON kbi_cmc.coursemoduleid = kbi_cm.id AND
                                                                        kbi_cmc.completionstate IN
                                                                        (1,
                                                                         2)
                         LEFT JOIN mdl_assign_submission kbi_s ON kbi_s.status = 'submitted' AND kbi_s.assignment = kbi_a.id
                         LEFT JOIN mdl_assign_grades kbi_g ON kbi_g.assignment = kbi_a.id
                  WHERE kbi_a.course > 0
                    AND kbi_a.course = :courseid
                  GROUP BY kbi_a.course) stat ON stat.courseid = kbi_c.id
       LEFT JOIN (SELECT kbi_l.userid, kbi_l.courseid, SUM(kbi_l.timespend) as timespend, SUM(kbi_l.visits) as visits
                  FROM mdl_local_kopere_bi_tracking kbi_l, mdl_modules kbi_m, mdl_course_modules kbi_cm
                  WHERE kbi_l.page = 'module'
                    and kbi_m.name = 'assign'
                    AND kbi_cm.id = kbi_l.param
                    AND kbi_cm.module = kbi_m.id
                    AND kbi_l.courseid = :courseid
                  GROUP BY kbi_l.userid, kbi_l.courseid) l ON l.userid = kbi_u.id AND l.courseid = kbi_c.id
WHERE kbi_ctx.contextlevel = 50
  AND kbi_c.id = :courseid
  AND kbi_u.deleted = 0
  AND kbi_u.suspended = 0
  AND kbi_u.username != 'guest'
  AND kbi_c.visible = 1
  AND kbi_ra.roleid = 3

Limitations and cautions

  • The assignments, completed, submissions, and grades indicators are aggregated for the entire course, not per teacher. The same totals may appear for multiple teachers in that course.
  • The completed count uses COUNT(DISTINCT coursemoduleid) for student completion states 1 and 2. It counts assignment modules with at least one recorded completion, not students who completed them or assignments graded by that teacher.
  • The submissions count uses COUNT(DISTINCT assignment) and therefore counts assignments with at least one submission, not the total number of student submissions.
  • The grades count also uses COUNT(DISTINCT assignment) on grading records. It does not represent the number of grades awarded or identify the grader.
  • Time and visits come from mdl_local_kopere_bi_tracking by user, course, and module. They depend on the plugin's data collection and do not prove educational follow-up.
  • The assignment subqueries do not filter by date range, module visibility, or submission status. Interpret their metrics as technical indicators.
  • The original file has no separate PostgreSQL variant. Check SQL compatibility and permissions on the database in use.
