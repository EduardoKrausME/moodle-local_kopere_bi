The 127 base reports are each listed once. The 40 PostgreSQL variants are database-specific versions of existing definitions, not conceptually new reports. Actual availability depends on the database engine, settings, and data in your Moodle site.

Interpretation and limitations

Daily and weekly aggregations may use UTC. Reports based on quiz attempts consider completed states, and grade history depends on Moodle's native data retention. Some individual reports limit queries to 500 rows; use course and date filters for extensive analyses.
