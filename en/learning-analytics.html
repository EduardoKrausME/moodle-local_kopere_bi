Learning Analytics

Six dashboards, aggregated snapshots, and configurable rules for identifying students who may need attention.

Dashboards in this category

  1. Overview: daily activity indicators and trends.
  2. Attention queue: risk classification and identified reasons.
  3. Course health: completion, progress, grades, and engagement.
  4. Student 360: an overview of each active course enrolment.
  5. Progress versus grade: comparison between progress and performance.
  6. Learning pace: identification of declining participation.

How the data is calculated

When a dashboard opens, Kopere BI queries student, course, and daily activity snapshot tables instead of scanning the entire logstore_standard_log on every visit. With the default schedule, data synchronization runs hourly at minute 10, and snapshots are refreshed hourly at minute 20.

Moodle events and data→Synchronization→Snapshots→Dashboards

These calculated indicators are not, on their own, evidence of disengagement, dropout, or effective learning.

Default parameters

Setting Default value
Moderate inactivity 14 days
High inactivity 30 days
Minimum actions 5
Low progress 25%
Grace period after enrolment 7 days
Analysis history 90 days

All these values can be changed under Site administration → Plugins → Local plugins → Kopere BI. Adjust them to the characteristics of each course before interpreting the attention queue.

Responsible use

Use trends and anomalies to guide educational interventions rather than automatically assigning educational risk to someone based solely on clicks, days without access, or grades. Review false positives, gaps in event logs, accessibility and connectivity constraints, and academic calendar considerations.
