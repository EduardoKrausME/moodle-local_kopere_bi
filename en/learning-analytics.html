Learning Analytics

Six dashboards, scheduled snapshots and configurable thresholds.

Six operational dashboards

  1. Overview - key metrics and daily activity trends.
  2. Attention queue - risk level and contributing reasons.
  3. Course health - completion, progress, grades and engagement.
  4. Student 360 - metrics for active course enrolments.
  5. Progress vs grade - relationships between advancement and results.
  6. Learning momentum - falling-participation detection.

How snapshots work

Instead of scanning logstore_standard_log whenever users open pages, the plugin reads aggregated learner, course and daily snapshot tables. The default schedule runs report-table synchronization hourly at minute 10 and snapshot building hourly at minute 20.

Moodle records→Sync→Snapshots→Dashboards

Default thresholds

Setting Default
Medium inactivity 14 days
High inactivity 30 days
Minimum actions 5
Low progress 25%
Enrolment grace period 7 days
Analysis history 90 days

These values can be changed in Site administration → Plugins → Local plugins → Kopere BI.

Interpret carefully

Risk scores support review and intervention; they are not proof that a learner is disengaged or will fail. Consider calendar, accessibility, connectivity, atypical usage and missing data before taking action.
